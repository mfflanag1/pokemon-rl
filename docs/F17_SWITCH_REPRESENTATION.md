# F17 — switching representation and coverage

Status: experiment completed; descriptive representation gate 0/3 seeds.

F17 completed (2026-10-25): replayed all 640 existing teacher games / 32,811 accepted decisions, then ran a fixed two-arm semantic-input canary with 12,288 BC updates and 1,572,864 row presentations. The descriptive representation gate passed in 0/3 seeds. Voluntary-switch agreement on the reused development probe was 3.27–7.37% for the zero-input control and 0.88–4.74% with explicit public type/move metadata. No new games or full-battle strength test occurred. All 28 focused checks and exhaustive update/prediction replay pass, including six original-next-update recoveries and 18 checkpoint comparisons. F10 remains the reference; full G2/G3/G4 and public strength remain open. Final reserved data, public servers and Prime were unused; GitHub remains held.

Next proposed step: test a larger, more diverse full-battle teacher corpus against the original corpus while keeping the original 928-feature model, balanced sampling and update budget fixed. Use a fresh development probe and preserve final reserved data. This tests data coverage separately; broader data is a candidate remedy, not an established one. No policy promotion or PPO continuation follows from this canary.

This is an adaptive exploratory diagnostic on previously used development states. The intervention changes effective input capacity and gradient clipping/Adam dynamics as well as exposing semantics; it does not identify a sole causal bottleneck. Nominal public type metadata omits dynamic move, item and ability modifiers. Teacher imitation is not optimal action truth or playing strength. All three seeds and every action family are retained.

## Audit findings

Replayed 512 original F15 training games and 128 original F16 probe games in 16.67 seconds. All 640 ended naturally, with zero cutoffs. All 32,811 accepted rows, before/after player fingerprints, actions, feature/mask/target linkage, outcomes and original identities match. No new trajectories were sampled.

Voluntary switching has only 363 training rows from 257 games, versus 82 probe rows from 57 games. All 82 probe destination/foe species pairs are absent from same-family training examples;78/82 active-own/foe pairs are also absent. There are 357 unique training destination/foe pairs. Type relationships can recur across different species pairs; this is a coverage measure, not proof that every tactical situation is novel.

Player-projection teacher reconstruction agrees on 362/363 train and 82/82 probe voluntary-switch targets. Across all action families it differs on 53/26,278 training and 22/6,533 probe rows. Restoring entry/type/boost groups reduces these counts to 18/11 but is partial; no projection ceiling or causal attribution follows. The one training switch mismatch (game 201, p2 request 28, Registeel versus Cramorant-Gulping, teacher switches to Altaria) is retained, not labeled an erroneous target.

No exact 928-feature-plus-mask duplicates, conflicting labels or train/probe exact-input overlaps were observed. High-dimensional exact uniqueness does not establish sufficient representation or rule out approximate ambiguity.

## Fixed canary and verification

Measured canary training/diagnostic phase: 82.27 seconds on local CPU, peak parent RSS 1227.12 MiB. This phase excludes the initial metadata preparation/source snapshot; the separate 640-game audit is reported above. No training or evaluation battles were generated by the canary. Both arms used identical 2,048-step-per-seed balanced teacher/old-rehearsal batches; all 6,144 paired batch indices were independently compared. There were 786,432 teacher presentations and 786,432 original rehearsal presentations.

The 847 new explicit public metadata features enter through 54,208 zero-initialized weights. Both arms allocate the same parameter shapes and begin with exactly the old function; the zero arm receives zeros in the added block. Semantic inputs change effective capacity and optimizer gradients; clipping occurred in only five of the 12,288 updates (four semantic, one control). The value-head weights remain exactly unchanged, although shared-trunk predictions can change. The original 128 rehearsal rows are reconstructed in sealed F7 order from F6 train episodes 0–7; duplicate examples retain their original multiplicity. The later 512-state tactical oracle remains evaluation-only.

All 28 focused checks pass on the final source/runtime inventory. Pinned Showdown build passed. Exhaustive replay repeats all 12,288 updates and every train/probe/tactical probability, compares 18 checkpoints and resumes six original before-final-update states. Production, verification and workflow exit 0. Eleven copied in-memory evidence corruptions are rejected. Independent review findings, failed preflights and corrected scope are preserved.
