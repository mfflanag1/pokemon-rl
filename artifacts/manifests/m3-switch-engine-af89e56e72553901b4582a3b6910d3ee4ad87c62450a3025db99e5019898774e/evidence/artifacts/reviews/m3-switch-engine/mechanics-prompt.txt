1: # switch-engine-1
2: 
3: The sealed `switch-v1` roster and twelve-turn cutoff remain the admitted scope.
4: This port adds explicit conditional chance, independent scalar and JAX transitions,
5: a bounded player-only observation and a four-action learner smoke after parity.
6: No old implementation or receipt is edited.
7: 
8: Original member slots A/B never reorder internally. State holds species IDs 0/1/2,
9: speed variants, HP, PP, active original slots, phase (0 joint, 1 replacement,
10: 2 terminal), turn, group and winner (-1 live, otherwise absolute winning seat).
11: Only the fainted side acts during replacement; -1 is the waiting action. A joint
12: step resolves both commitments atomically. Switches precede moves; within the same
13: priority class faster outgoing active Pokémon acts first, with explicit tie bit.
14: A fainted actor does not execute or spend PP. Finish replacement before advancing
15: the turn. Terminal/cutoff states are absorbing in the batched kernel; checked host
16: submission rejects another active decision. Imported arbitrary states are not a
17: supported resume format: checkpoints bind and restore the complete reachable state.
18: 
19: History has 32 padded rows of nine integers:
20: `turn, actor, kind, member, species, move, affected_member, HP_after, crit`.
21: Kind 0 is a switch, kind 1 an executed damaging move. Species is 0/1/2;
22: move is 0/1 or -1 for a switch. HP is the switched-in member's HP for switches
23: and the opponent target's HP for moves. Initial send-outs are represented in the
24: initial observation, not in history. No damage roll is actor-visible. The player
25: projection converts actor to relative 0=self/1=opponent and HP to exact own or
26: ceil(100*HP/maxHP) for opponents (clamp 100 to 99 when HP is below max); unused history rows are -1. Faint is implicit in
27: HP zero. Every reachable event fits: at most 24 joint actions plus two replacement
28: switches. Terminal winner labels are absent from the actor input.
29:

31: special attack, special defense, speed, active flag; pp[2,2]; opponent[2,4] =
32: species+1 (0 unknown), public HP percent (-1 unknown), active flag, fainted flag;

36: the observer's executed moves, including while waiting; own HP incorporates private
