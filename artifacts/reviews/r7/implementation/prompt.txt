171: def partition(payload, policy):
172:     """Assign the team to a split using the full unsigned 256-bit salted digest.
173: 
174:     Thresholds are compared as exact integers: ``digest * denominator`` is
175:     placed against ``bound * 2**256``. No floats and no modulo reduction.

184:         raise ValueError('digest outside the unsigned 256-bit range')
